Loyalty Strategy
Feb 2026 – Mar 2026

Vue Cinemas — Loyalty Strategy Proposal

Problem: Vue Cinemas, a challenger cinema brand in the Netherlands, wanted to build genuine customer loyalty rather than relying on customers who simply had no other nearby cinema to choose from.

Approach: Designed and ran a survey (84 respondents) on cinema choice and booking behavior, then analyzed the relationship between Vue's Movie Pass membership, visit frequency and spend.

Recommendation: Found location, not the loyalty program, as the #1 driver of cinema choice (quality was #2). Movie Pass membership was associated with higher visit frequency, but not higher spend, leading to a recommendation for a location-segmented loyalty strategy over a blanket keep-or-drop decision.

Market Entry
Oct 2025 – Dec 2025

ClayTec — Dutch Market Entry Strategy

Problem: ClayTec had scaled from a craftsman workshop into standardized products without ever formally defining its market positioning, target segments, or competitive landscape.

Approach: Worked on market segmentation, competitor analysis and stakeholder mapping (homeowner → architect → contractor → wholesaler → installer), and built the go-to-market roadmap and brand positioning.

Solved: Delivered a 3-phase go-to-market roadmap for the Dutch residential renovation segment, with a concrete distribution approach and clear positioning ("ultra-low CO₂, fully circular, naturally healthier") in place of a generic strategy.

Customer Experience
Oct 2025 – Dec 2025

Reinventing the Gifting Experience for a Dutch Jewelry Retailer

Problem: The retailer's gifting journey, its most important sales occasion, lacked any emotional connection: no storytelling online, generic packaging, no personalized help in-store.

Approach: Diagnosed the journey gaps, designed a prototype (a redesigned gift box with a QR code linking to a personal voice message from the gift-giver, plus an in-store recording booth), and tested it with a customer survey.

Solved: The new packaging concept scored significantly higher than the current one on feeling personal (+24%), memorable (+18%), and better overall (+49%).
